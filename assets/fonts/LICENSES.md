# Bundled fonts

These files are redistributed with śikṣāmitra. Generated by
`tools/fonts/fetch.mjs` from `tools/fonts/manifest.mjs` — do not edit.

Shipping a font inside an installer is redistribution, so every family here is
under a licence that permits it, and the notice travels with the file.

## OFL-1.1

- **Gentium Book Plus** (`gentium-book-plus`) — text
  The scholar's face for IAST. Designed for exactly this: heavy diacritic stacking that stays legible at reading size.
- **EB Garamond** (`eb-garamond`) — text
  Classical and light on the page. Small x-height, so it wants more leading than the others.
- **Source Serif 4** (`source-serif-4`) — text
  Built for screens. Sturdier than Garamond at small sizes and more even in colour, which suits a dense page.
- **Crimson Pro** (`crimson-pro`) — text
  Book-like and warm, with a calm rhythm. The quietest of the four.
- **Noto Serif Devanagari** (`noto-serif-devanagari`) — deva
  Full conjunct coverage and a serif weight that sits beside the Latin text faces without looking pasted in.
- **Noto Serif Telugu** (`noto-serif-telugu`) — telu
  The corpus's Telugu is verified against these forms.
- **Noto Serif Tamil** (`noto-serif-tamil`) — taml
  Tamil forms in the corpus are UNREVIEWED; the face is shipped so they render, not as a claim that they are right.
- **Inter** (`inter`) — ui
  Neutral to the point of invisibility, which is what chrome should be. Excellent at 11–12px, where a tool lives.
- **IBM Plex Sans** (`ibm-plex-sans`) — ui
  What v1 used. Slightly technical, a little warmer than Inter.
- **Source Sans 3** (`source-sans-3`) — ui
  Humanist and gentle — the softest of the three, for a quieter shell.
- **IBM Plex Mono** (`ibm-plex-mono`) — mono
  For the source view and anywhere a codepoint is shown.
