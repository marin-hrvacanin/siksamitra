Privacy · 10 September 2026

What śikṣāmitra does with your text

Nothing. It has no server to send it to.

That is the whole policy, and the rest of this page is only the detail. Both the program and the Word add-in run entirely on your own machine.

The Word add-in

The task pane is a static web page. Word loads it, and from then on everything it does happens between the pane and the document open in front of you. It reads the paragraph the caret is in, works out the markings, and writes the paragraph back.

  • Your document, or any part of it, is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Nothing is stored — not on a server, not in the browser, not on disk.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no licence check.
  • There is no analytics, no telemetry and no error reporting.
  • No cookies are set.

The page itself is fetched from wherever you installed it from — GitHub Pages, vedaunion.org, or your own computer — and that server sees an ordinary request for a web page: a date, an address, a browser name. Nothing about your document is in it. If you would rather not make even that request, the add-in can be served from your own machine; the instructions are on the install page.

Word itself loads Microsoft's office.js from Microsoft's servers, because that is the bridge every Office add-in is built on and Microsoft requires the production copy rather than a pinned one. What Microsoft does with that request is covered by their own privacy statement, not this one.

The desktop program

The same answer. Documents are files on your disk; there is no synchronisation and no cloud. It can optionally sign in to vedaunion.org to fetch or publish a document, and that is a deliberate act each time — nothing happens until you ask for it, and the program works completely without it.

If any of that changes

It will be said here first, with a date, and the program is open source: the claim above is checkable rather than promised. Every network request either program can make is in the source, and there are not many.
